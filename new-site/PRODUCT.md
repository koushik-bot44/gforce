# G-Force IT: product brief

**Company:** G-FORCE IT SERVICES INC., 130 King Street West, Suite 1900, Toronto,
ON M5X 1E3. Confirm published contact details and support hours with G-Force.

**Register:** brand/marketing. Presentation is part of the company's credibility.

## Audiences and actions

| Audience | Need | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| SMB owner or office manager | Dependable managed IT, cybersecurity, cloud/backup and consulting | Request a free IT assessment or call |
| Career switcher or learner | Practical IT skills, suitable course and clear next steps | Explore training and enquire about a course |

Training covers cloud, AI/ML, cybersecurity, data analytics and DevOps, with
virtual, in-person and self-paced formats described in the course data.

## Current presentation

Keep the original supplied logo and its original crimson palette. The glass
sculpture follows the original G-FORCE wordmark outlines. Air contributes the
cloud atmosphere and scroll-driven rotation/shrink/lift; it does not replace
G-Force's identity. Archivo is the interface typeface.

Business and learner paths must be easy to find. The complete name, headings,
training section, actions and contact details must remain readable across
mobile/desktop, delayed JavaScript, restored scroll positions and motion fallbacks.

## Content rules

- Do not publish invented or unverified statistics, testimonials, case studies,
  client relationships or partner credentials. Current sample proof is archived
  in source and omitted from public pages, including the 90% outages claim.
- Do not present stock people as G-Force employees or customer testimonial authors.
  Use licensed imagery and preserve the credits.
- Describe certification exams as course preparation, not accreditation or a
  partnership. Confirm course prices, schedules and applicable registration status.
- Dawn tabs describe service routines rather than a live activity feed or a
  demonstrated customer result. Avoid public Example/Replay presentation.
- Use professional, specific English and Canadian spelling. Remove decorative
  dots and implementation commentary from visitor-facing content.
- Forms include privacy/consent text; newsletter consent stays separate and unticked.

## Delivery and quality

Forms submit JSON only when `PUBLIC_FORM_ENDPOINT` is configured. Otherwise they
explicitly open an email draft. Booking uses `PUBLIC_BOOKING_URL` when provided,
or an email fallback. Verify the real provider before reporting successful
server delivery or calendar integration.

Targets are WCAG 2.2 AA, mobile LCP ≤2.5 seconds and smooth purposeful motion.
These are goals, not certifications or current measured results. Evaluate the
latest build with browser interactions, moving sequences and reproducible
Lighthouse conditions. Keep reduced-motion, Save-Data and unsupported-WebGL
paths complete. Start live WebGL only after visitor intent and avoid rendering
an idle sculpture continuously.

Remaining client inputs: confirmed contact/hours, course details and status,
form endpoint, booking URL, and permissioned/substantiated proof if it is to be
published later. Feature status is tracked in `REQUIREMENTS.md`.
